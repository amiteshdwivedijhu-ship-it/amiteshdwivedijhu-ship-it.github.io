# MSK Steerage Desk

Unlisted static prototype. Open `index.html`. No build step and no network calls.

Synthetic health plan members, shown as initials and age bands only. No PHI. Not an Icon Health product.

This is not a claims system, an eligibility check, or a scheduler. The hard rule blocks a "Steered" mark when the provider score is under 4 of 5, or when the reason and the member's agreement are missing. Claims older than 30 days stay under fresh members unless a coordinator writes an override note.
