# Party Ready Check

A working concept prototype. A host sets a play time for a fake game, invites friends, and sees who is ready across phone and a console card. Fixable items have a one-tap action. Nothing is purchased. Remote download runs only after that friend confirms, and only if their console allows it.

This is not a Sony Interactive Entertainment or PlayStation product. Players, the game, and the numbers are synthetic. Features here are a concept.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step. Reset demo restores the seed. A timer treats 1 second as 1 minute. Fast-forward jumps 25 minutes.

## 90-second path

1. Create Starfall Rally at 8:00 and invite Ren, Mira, Theo, and Ava.
2. The board shows 1 Ready, 3 Fixable, and 1 Not ready.
3. Kai starts the download to the PS5. The screen names Rest Mode. Send Theo a reminder.
4. Open Theo's phone card and tap Download to my console now.
5. On Ava's card, tap Join later. Fast-forward the clock. The board is ready except Ava, who is joining later.
6. The TV card shows 4 of 5 ready. Start together unlocks.
7. Open the PM view for the funnel and the 4T nudge fields.

## Start rule

Start together stays locked until every invited friend is Ready or has chosen Join later. A lapsed membership shows a plain note and one link. There is no purchase button.
