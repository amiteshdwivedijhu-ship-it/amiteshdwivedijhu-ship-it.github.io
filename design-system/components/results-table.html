The data layer is never glass

Everything measured sits in a flat well: no blur, no gloss, no hover lift, no entrance animation. The one thing on the page that does not move is the evidence. Values are generated at build time from runs/scorecards/_summary.json and are never typed by hand.

Gates are pass or fail, and are reported before any utility metric. No utility metric offsets a failed gate. Gate: met-precision at or above 0.95 with a lower Wilson bound above 0.90.
